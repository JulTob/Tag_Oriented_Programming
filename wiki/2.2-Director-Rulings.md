# 2.2 — The Director's Rulings: a Register

> Every design decision of Tag-Oriented Programming is made by the Director
> and recorded on the record: *"Nothing is decided in silence"*
> (`steps/README.md`). In practice those records live in three kinds of
> places — the `### Decision` blocks of `steps/spec/STEP-SPEC-*.md`, issue
> threads that transcribe a choice, and STEP Briefs riding open pull
> requests. This page gathers **every ruling in one table**, with where it
> is recorded and whether today's `main` implements it.
>
> Compiled 2026-10-20 against `main` at `30dfe49` and re-verified by probes;
> each status was checked against the code, not assumed from the paperwork.
> This register is a **map, not an authority**: when it disagrees with a
> STEP's own Decision block, the STEP wins, and a correction here is owed.

## Who rules, and where rulings live

- Julio Toboso (@JulTob) is the **Director**: steward and, for now, the final design
  authority over the Specification (GOVERNANCE.md:7). All merges to `main` require the
  Director's review (GOVERNANCE.md:23, CODEOWNERS).
- A STEP moves from **Vetting** to **Cleared** or **Redacted** only by the Director, who
  "writes the reason into the STEP. Nothing is decided in silence" (steps/README.md:22).
  Lifecycle: Recon → Brief → Vetting → Cleared/Redacted → Deployed (steps/README.md:13–20).
- Rulings are recorded in three places: the `### Decision` block (and dated amendment
  notes) of each `steps/spec/STEP-SPEC-*.md`; issue bodies/comments that transcribe a
  decision (e.g. #30, #36, #71, #74, #233); and STEP Briefs inside open PRs that record a
  ruling before clearance (STEP-SPEC-22 on PR #171; STEP-SPEC-45 on PR #232; STEP-SPEC-46
  on PR #235).
- The Director's category-theory notes
  (the Director's category-theory notes (see wiki chapter 2.1), dated 15 Oct 2026 against commit
  `b2f6ef9`) are analysis, not rulings: they contain no "Decision" and change no STEP
  status. They are excluded from the table below.

## Status legend

- **On main** — the ruling's behavior is on today's `main` (test file or probe named).
- **On main; record lags** — behavior is on main but the STEP's status/Decision block
  was never updated by the Director.
- **Open PR #N** — the ruling's implementation (or its only record) lives in an open PR.
- **Design record** — ruled and recorded, but no runtime on main implements it yet.
- **Superseded** — a later ruling replaced it; kept on the record.
